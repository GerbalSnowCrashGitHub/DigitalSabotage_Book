Modern Digital Sabotage: How Hacktivism, AI, and Cyberwarfare Are Reshaping the World
By Chris Kubecka – “The Hacktress”

🧠 The truth isn't just under attack—it's being rewritten in real time.

Welcome to the age of Generative AI manipulation, digital sabotage, and hybrid warfare, where deepfakes sway elections, IoT weapons sabotage nations, and rogue states partner with cartels to weaponize your infrastructure.

Cybersecurity expert Chris Kubecka—known worldwide as The Hacktress—delivers a gripping, highly accessible guide through today’s most urgent cyber battlefields. Drawing from real-world operations, war game scenarios, and her own frontline experience across governments, militaries, and critical infrastructure, this book is part manual, part manifesto, and entirely essential.
🔥 What’s Inside:

    AI as a Friend—for Your Enemy: How artificial intelligence is used in influence operations and authoritarian control.

    Weaponizing IoT: Drones, satellites, surveillance systems, and even medical devices vulnerable to sabotage.

    Digital Sabotage Playbooks: Step-by-step breakdowns of how activists and state actors exploit DNS, VoIP, and smart infrastructure.

    Hacktivism Meets Humor: “Easter eggs” and satirical nods to surveillance states, misinformation memes, and tech dystopias.

    Case Studies: Including Moldova, Venezuela (Arawakland), Trump 2.0 influence campaigns, and AI-powered protests.

Whether you're a cybersecurity pro, journalist, policy advisor, tech ethics enthusiast, or just AI-curious, this book unpacks complex global threats with sharp insight, technical clarity, and a rebel spirit.
📚 Bonus Features:

    Glossary of 150+ cutting-edge terms: From GENINT to Zero-Day GPT, get up to speed fast.

    Tactical Indexing: Designed for real-world use by red teams, researchers, and rebels alike.

    Behind-the-scenes: Learn how this book was crafted under digital suppression and geopolitical tension.
